Credits & Honours

The Nekoropolis could not have raised its banners without the work of its clans.

Every word and every line of code was hammered out at the Ember Forge by Grom Ashpelt, who would like it known that he only shouted at the stylesheet twice. Design and illustrations were painted by Vashti Inkskin. Visitors were interviewed, loudly, by Mika Emberpaw. If you spot an error, send word and Grom will reforge it.

(Between us: each of those clan-names is Tobi, wearing different war-paint.)

The Wandering Spirit

Special thanks to a wandering spirit called Claude, an AI assistant made by Anthropic. It was consulted at the Ember Forge, never once took a sun-nap, and could not be beaten at arguing about headings. In plain terms, here is what each AI tool did:

  • Planning: Claude Opus 5.5 helped draft the site plan (plan.md). Read the planning chat.
  • Plan review: Claude reviewed the plan against the project requirements. Read the review chat.
  • Implementation: Claude Opus 5.5 wrote the HTML, CSS and menu script, drafted the in-universe copy, and drew the placeholder art, map and animation in code. Read the build chat.
  • Final check: Claude checked the finished site against the plan's definition of done. Read the final-check chat.

Tobi reviewed, edited and approved everything the spirit produced.
