Welcome, Traveller

You stand at the gates of the Nekoropolis, red-stone city of the Nekoro and home of eleven proud clans. We are hunters, smiths, storytellers and champions, and we are very good at all of it. We see no reason to pretend otherwise.

Our first law is the law of the hearth: a guest who shares our fire is protected by every claw in the city. So come in, warm your paws, eat until you can't, and stay for the stories. Just never wake a sleeping Nekoro in the afternoon. The sun belongs to whoever is lying in it.

A Royal Proclamation
