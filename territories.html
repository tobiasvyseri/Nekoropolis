Territories of the Nekoropolis

Our city climbs from the river to the red cliffs. Here is where to go, and how not to embarrass yourself once you get there.

The Sunthrone

The great hall of the King, carved into a cliff of red rock. Petitions are heard at noon, judgements at dusk.

Visitor's tip: Bow to the throne, not the king. He prefers it that way.

The Proving Ring

A sand arena at the heart of the city. Duels, trials of rank and the loudest festivals in the land.

Visitor's tip: Watchers cheer for both fighters. Booing a loser is the only real foul.

The Shrine of the Sun-Eye

The High Seer's temple: a stone spire capped in gold that catches the first light.

Visitor's tip: Come at dawn. Ask one question. Expect a riddle back.

Hearthfire Hall

The feast hall that never closes. Guests eat first, always.

Visitor's tip: Claws sheathed at the hearth. Arguments wait until the plates are empty.

The Kittenfold

Where young Nekoro are raised together and taught to climb, spar and boast properly.

Visitor's tip: Visitors are welcome. Expect to be climbed.

Scratchstone Cliffs

Claw-scarred climbing cliffs where warriors train their grip and nerve.

Visitor's tip: Add your own mark low on the rock. The high marks belong to champions.

The Sunning Steppes

Warm golden grassland for the dawn hunt and the sacred afternoon nap.

Visitor's tip: Never wake a napper. Find your own patch of sun.

The Ember Forge

The smithy district. Our library is our weapons, and the stories told about each one.

Visitor's tip: Ask any blade’s name and you will hear its whole history.

Fishgate Docks

The river market on the Fang River. The busiest place in the city at dawn.

Visitor's tip: Arrive before sunrise for the best trout.

The Featherwall

The neutral border with Aviaria, where Nekoro and Avianis meet to play the Great Game instead of going to war.

Visitor's tip: Win loudly, lose honestly, and shake the Feathered by the wingtip after.
