A Visitor's Guide

Places of Note
Place What happens there Visitor's tip
The Sunthrone The great hall of the King, carved into a cliff of red rock. Petitions are heard at noon, judgements at dusk. Bow to the throne, not the king. He prefers it that way.
The Proving Ring A sand arena at the heart of the city. Duels, trials of rank and the loudest festivals in the land. Watchers cheer for both fighters. Booing a loser is the only real foul.
The Shrine of the Sun-Eye The High Seer's temple: a stone spire capped in gold that catches the first light. Come at dawn. Ask one question. Expect a riddle back.
Hearthfire Hall The feast hall that never closes. Guests eat first, always. Claws sheathed at the hearth. Arguments wait until the plates are empty.
The Kittenfold Where young Nekoro are raised together and taught to climb, spar and boast properly. Visitors are welcome. Expect to be climbed.
Scratchstone Cliffs Claw-scarred climbing cliffs where warriors train their grip and nerve. Add your own mark low on the rock. The high marks belong to champions.
The Sunning Steppes Warm golden grassland for the dawn hunt and the sacred afternoon nap. Never wake a napper. Find your own patch of sun.
The Ember Forge The smithy district. Our library is our weapons, and the stories told about each one. Ask any blade’s name and you will hear its whole history.
Fishgate Docks The river market on the Fang River. The busiest place in the city at dawn. Arrive before sunrise for the best trout.
The Featherwall The neutral border with Aviaria, where Nekoro and Avianis meet to play the Great Game instead of going to war. Win loudly, lose honestly, and shake the Feathered by the wingtip after.

Training in the Proving Ring

Do Not Miss

The Proving Ring

Every quarrel in the city ends here, one way or another. King Rhazan won his crown on this sand, and any Nekoro may challenge for rank on festival days. Visitors may challenge too. Visitors usually lose. Everyone still goes to the feast.

Hearthfire Hall

The fires here have not gone out in three hundred years. Guests eat first, champions eat second, and the king eats whenever Grom the smith lets him through the door.

The Sunning Steppes

Golden grass to the horizon. We hunt here at dawn and sleep here all afternoon. Interrupting a sun-nap is the rudest thing a guest can do, so tread softly.

The Featherwall

Our border with Aviaria. The Avianis hid in their sky-city for generations; now they come down to play the Great Game with us on neutral stone. They are cunning players. We are louder ones. Try a game yourself (external site).
